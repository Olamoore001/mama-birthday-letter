What You Mean To Me Ma

Dear Mama, ❤

I have been thinking about how to properly express what you mean to me ma, and honestly,I don't think a few words could ever fully express it.

There are people who pass through our lives, and there are people whose presence becomes part of our story. You are one of those people for me, Ma — someone I have come to see as second only to my own mother.

You are not simply my pastor's wife. You have become someone I genuinely see as a motherly figure in my life. Someone who cares about me, checks on me, encourages me, corrects me, prays for me, and genuinely wants to see me become better.

You have been there for different parts of my life. You have cared for me physically, emotionally, spiritually, academically, and even in the journey of discovering and developing the gifts and skills God has placed in me.

You have encouraged my education. You have encouraged my web development. You have encouraged my graphic design. You have encouraged me to keep learning, keep growing, and keep becoming better.

But, Ma, what means even more to me is knowing that your care for me goes beyond what people may see from the outside.
There have been many times when I didn't understand what God was trying to communicate to me, and somehow, He would use you to help me understand. There have been moments when you would tell me something, counsel me, correct me, or give me direction about something I hadn't even told you. And those moments have made me realize that your place in my life is not something I should take for granted.

I believe God has deliberately placed you in my life for a reason. You have become one of the people He uses to watch over me, guide me, encourage me, correct me, and remind me of the person He is helping me become.

So when I think about what you mean to me, I don't just think about the things you have done for me. I think about the kind of person you have been to me and the impact your presence has had on my life.

You are someone whose presence has become part of my story.

And for that, Ma, I am deeply grateful. ❤️

Thank You Ma.

There are some things I simply want you to know Ma.

Thank you for your care.

Thank you for your kindness.

Thank you for your prayers.

Thank you for your encouragement.

Thank you for being someone I genuinely appreciate.

Some people fill a role in your life. Others fill a place in your heart. You have done the second, and I don't take that for granted ma. Thank you for every prayer said quietly on my behalf, and for every word of encouragement that came at exactly the right time.

Before I finish…

This is my prayer for you Ma.

Father, I thank You for the gift of her life.

Thank You for the woman she is, for her heart, her strength, her kindness and the love she gives to others.

As she begins another year, may You continue to guide her, strengthen her and surround her with Your presence.

May this new year bring her peace, joy, wisdom and beautiful reasons to be grateful.

May You preserve her, bless the work of her hands and cause her to experience the goodness of Your promises.

May she continue to grow in grace and remain a blessing to everyone around her.

In Jesus' name.

Amen. ❤
